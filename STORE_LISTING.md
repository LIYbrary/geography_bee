# Google Play store listing

Ready to paste into Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**.
Written for **long-tail searches**: specific phrases a parent, teacher or student actually types, where a new app can reach the top results. Broad words ("reading app", "quiz", "study app") are left out on purpose until the app has ratings.

Play rules to keep: no keyword lists or repeated keywords, no "#1/best/top" claims, no other apps' names, no unattributed reviews. Google also reads the full description for search, so the phrases appear in normal sentences.

## Full description (1086/4000)
```
Geography Bee Practice helps students get ready for a school or regional geography bee. It's free, with no ads and no accounts.

Practice with questions across 16 topics:
• Capitals and flags of every country
• US state capitals, and US & Canada facts
• Continents, oceans, rivers, mountains and deserts
• Neighbors and borders between countries
• World regions: Africa, Asia, Europe, Oceania & Antarctica, Latin America & the Caribbean
• Landmarks, languages and currencies
• Geography terms

Choose easy, medium or hard, multiple choice or type-in answers, and 5 to 50 questions. The timed bee round works like a real geography bee: type each answer against a 20-second clock. Missed questions go into a review list, and your streaks and scores are saved on your device.

Good for elementary and middle school students, geography clubs and anyone who wants a world capitals quiz or flags quiz on the go. Works offline once installed, except voice answers and flags you haven't seen yet.

Geography Bee Practice is part of LIYbrary, the Learn It Yourself library of free practice apps.
```
