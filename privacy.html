Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Geography Bee Practice does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party services.

Scores, streaks and missed questions are saved only on your device (browser local storage). You can erase them with "Reset all progress" or by clearing the app's data.

The app works offline once loaded. Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/lopa23.
