Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Geography Bee Practice does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

Flag pictures are loaded from flagcdn.com, which, like any website, sees the device's IP address when an image is requested. No other information is sent.

If you tap the microphone button to answer by voice, your device's built-in speech recognition turns your speech into text. On some devices (for example Chrome on Android) this audio is processed by Google under Google's privacy policy. Geography Bee never records or keeps the audio. The microphone is only used while the button is active, and typing works without it.

Scores, streaks and missed questions are saved only on your device (browser local storage). You can erase them with "Reset all progress" or by clearing the app's data.

The app works offline once loaded. Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/LIYbrary/geography_bee.
